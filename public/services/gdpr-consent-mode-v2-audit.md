### GDPR / Consent Mode V2 Compliance Audit

**Is your consent setup quietly costing you ad data, or exposing you to risk?**

Consent Mode v2 has been mandatory for advertisers targeting EEA/UK users since March 2024. But on 15 June 2026, Google changed the rules again: consent signals are now the sole gate for Google Ads data flowing through GA4. If your consent setup has any gaps, you likely lost ad conversion data that week without knowing it. This audit finds out exactly where you stand.

**Timeline:** 2–10 days &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Day rate or fixed package, report + walkthrough call

#### What's Included

- Full review of your consent platform and how it maps to Google's four consent signals (`ad_storage`, `analytics_storage`, `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`).
- A check that GTM and GA4 are correctly respecting user consent choices.
- An assessment of how the 15 June 2026 Google Ads change has affected your data.
- A light review of your privacy notice/cookie policy against what's actually being collected.
- A risk-rated findings report and fix roadmap.

#### Timeline

2–4 days for day-rate engagements, or up to 10 business days for the fixed package, depending on how many markets or brands are in scope.

#### Investment

Available as a day rate for ad hoc or exploratory scope, or a fixed package for a defined single-market or multi-market/multi-brand review, including bundled with the [Tagging & Tracking Audit](/services/tagging-tracking-audit). Get in touch below for a quote.

#### Best Fit For

- Businesses advertising to EEA/UK audiences via Google Ads.
- Teams who saw a drop in Google Ads conversion data since mid-June 2026 and haven't diagnosed why.
- Companies in regulated or reputationally sensitive sectors where a compliance gap is a real business risk, not just a data-quality one.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Admin or read access to your consent management platform (CMP).
- Read access to GTM and GA4.
- A list of ad platforms currently in use, and sight of your current privacy notice/cookie policy.
