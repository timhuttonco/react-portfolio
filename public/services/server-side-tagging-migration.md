### Server-Side Tagging Migration

**Better data fidelity, faster pages, and stronger privacy compliance, moved off the client and onto infrastructure you control.**

Client-side tags are increasingly unreliable: ad blockers, browser tracking prevention, and third-party cookie restrictions all chip away at the data they can capture. Moving tag management server-side (via server-side Google Tag Manager on Cloud Run, or equivalent infrastructure) puts you back in control of what's collected and how it's enriched, while materially improving conversion signal quality for ad platforms. I directed exactly this migration across Kingfisher plc's full multi-brand digital estate, alongside a parallel migration from Tealium to GTM.

**Timeline:** Scoped to estate size &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Planning, migration, and validation

#### What's Included

- Architecture planning for a server-side tagging environment (Google Cloud Run or equivalent), sized to your traffic and budget.
- Migration plan sequencing client-side tags to server-side equivalents with minimal reporting disruption.
- First-party cookie and domain configuration to maximise data retention against browser restrictions.
- Data enrichment opportunities identified (e.g. server-side deduplication, hashing, IP/geo enrichment).
- Post-migration validation confirming data continuity against pre-migration benchmarks.

#### Best Fit For

- Mid-size to large businesses seeing declining conversion tracking accuracy due to browser privacy changes.
- Multi-brand or multi-market estates where consistent, centrally-managed tagging infrastructure would reduce duplicated effort.
- Teams who've outgrown client-side-only tagging and want a partner who has actually delivered this migration before, not just diagrammed it.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Access to current tag management platform (GTM, Tealium, or other) and hosting/DNS admin, or a technical contact who has it.
- Sight of current traffic volumes and peak load patterns, to size infrastructure correctly.
- Stakeholder alignment on acceptable migration windows/downtime, if any.
