### CDP / Tealium AudienceStream Engineering

**Hands-on AudienceStream build and roadmap ownership, from audience segmentation to activation.**

Direct experience on both sides of this platform: I set up the AudienceStream implementation methodology and QA standards while managing Tealium's EMEA delivery team, and more recently owned AudienceStream's development and roadmap in-house as a client at Kingfisher plc: audience segmentation, identity resolution, and activation into marketing channels, alongside the wider Tealium iQ and EventStream estate.

For the ad-platform tagging side of activation, see [Marketing Tag Implementation](/services/marketing-tag-implementation); for broader ownership of the wider customer data platform roadmap, see [Data Product Manager / Product Owner](/services/data-product-manager).

**Timeline:** Project-based build or ongoing engagement &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Day rate or fixed scope

#### What's Included

- AudienceStream build and configuration: audience/segment builds, identity resolution setup, and connector (action) configuration for activation into marketing and ad platforms.
- Roadmap ownership: prioritising segmentation and activation use cases with marketing and MarTech stakeholders, tied into a wider customer data platform strategy spanning Web, App and CRM.
- First-party data and identity strategy: server-side data collection, consent-aware capture, and profile stitching across devices and channels.
- Data activation: connector/action deployments to platforms like Meta CAPI and Google Enhanced Conversions, improving match rates and signal quality for personalisation and paid media.
- Tealium iQ and EventStream tag management alongside AudienceStream, including implementation methodology and deployment QA standards.

#### Best Fit For

- Businesses running (or evaluating) Tealium AudienceStream who need hands-on build capacity or roadmap ownership, not just advisory input.
- Marketing or MarTech teams whose audience segmentation and activation work has stalled without dedicated CDP engineering support.
- Companies who've seen ad platform match rates or personalisation quality suffer and suspect their identity resolution or activation setup is the cause.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Access to your Tealium AudienceStream and iQ instance (or a sandbox/trial for build work).
- Sight of current segmentation and activation use cases, and the marketing/MarTech stakeholders who own them.
- Access to the ad and marketing platforms audiences need to activate into (Meta Business Manager, Google Ads, etc.).
