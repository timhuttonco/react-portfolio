### Marketing Tag Implementation

**Get every ad platform tracking correctly, consent-gated, and feeding usable retargeting audiences.**

Ad platforms are only as good as the data feeding them. A Meta Pixel or LinkedIn Insight Tag that's badly implemented, firing before consent, or double-counting conversions quietly wastes ad spend and skews optimisation. This service gets your marketing tags implemented properly from the start, recently delivered as a full stack for a UK design agency, covering GA4, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, and LinkedIn Insight Tag, all managed through GTM with GDPR-compliant consent gating.

If your audiences also need to be built and activated through a CDP rather than just tagged, see [CDP / Tealium AudienceStream Engineering](/services/cdp-tealium-audiencestream-engineering).

**Timeline:** 1–2 weeks &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Implementation + handover documentation

#### What's Included

- Implementation of the ad platform tags you need (Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, TikTok Pixel, and others) via Google Tag Manager.
- Consent-gated firing rules integrated with your existing CMP (or recommendations for one if you don't have one).
- Conversion event mapping aligned to your actual business goals, not just default page-view tracking.
- Retargeting audience setup across platforms, synced where possible for consistent cross-channel targeting.
- Full handover documentation (plus a recorded walkthrough) so your team can extend the setup independently.

#### Best Fit For

- Businesses running paid campaigns across multiple ad platforms who want unified, trustworthy conversion data.
- Teams launching a new site, landing page, or campaign who want tracking built correctly from day one.
- Anyone who suspects their retargeting audiences are smaller or less accurate than they should be.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Admin access to GTM (or willingness to grant it during the project).
- Access to the ad accounts (Meta Business Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, etc.) that need tagging.
- A contact who can confirm the conversion events that matter most to the business.
