### Fractional Analytics & Data Product Leadership

**Senior measurement leadership, on tap, without the overhead of a full-time hire.**

Not every business is ready for a full-time Head of Analytics or Data Product Manager, but plenty need that level of ownership: a measurement roadmap tied to commercial goals, a team that's actually managed rather than just supported, and someone accountable when data quality or compliance questions land on a leadership team's desk. This is the role I've played full-time at Kingfisher plc, owning the measurement function across an international multi-brand estate, offered here on a fractional, retained basis for businesses that need the same oversight at a fraction of the cost.

If what you need is more hands-on (embedded ownership of a single data product rather than oversight of a whole function), see [Data Product Manager / Product Owner](/services/data-product-manager) instead.

**Timeline:** Ongoing, typically 1–2 days per week or per month &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Retainer

#### What's Included

- A measurement strategy and rolling roadmap aligned to your commercial KPIs, not just a list of tracking tickets.
- Hands-on or advisory oversight of an implementation team (in-house, agency, or contractors), depending on what you already have.
- Ongoing GDPR/consent compliance oversight as regulations and Google's own enforcement continue to shift.
- Regular reporting to leadership on data quality, roadmap progress, and risk.
- Direct availability for the technical or strategic questions that come up between formal check-ins.

#### Best Fit For

- Growing businesses that have outgrown ad hoc analytics support but aren't ready for a full-time senior hire.
- Companies who've just completed an audit (tagging, GDPR, or otherwise) and want ongoing ownership rather than a one-off fix.
- Teams who need a credible senior voice on measurement in the room with leadership or legal, on a regular basis.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Visibility of current team structure and existing analytics/tagging setup.
- A named internal stakeholder as the primary point of contact.
- Clarity on the commercial goals the measurement function is expected to support.
