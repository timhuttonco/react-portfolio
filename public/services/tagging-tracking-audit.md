### Tagging & Tracking Audit

**Find out what your GA4 and GTM setup is actually telling you, and fix what's wrong.**

If nobody has audited your analytics setup since it was built, there's a good chance you're making decisions on broken data: duplicate events inflating your numbers, conversions that were never mapped correctly, or tags firing before, or without, user consent. This audit tells you exactly where, and what to do about it.

**Timeline:** 1–2 weeks &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Fixed-fee, report + walkthrough call

#### What's Included

- Full review of your GA4 property configuration, events, and conversions.
- Full review of your GTM container: tags, triggers, and firing order.
- A check that tags are correctly respecting user consent.
- A written report, ranked by severity, with plain-English fixes.
- A 30–45 minute walkthrough call to talk through the findings together.

#### Timeline

1–2 weeks from kickoff to delivered report, depending on setup complexity. Smaller, single-property setups land at the front of that window; multi-market or multi-brand setups land at the back.

#### Investment

Priced as a flat fee, agreed up front once scope is confirmed, so there are no hourly billing surprises. Get in touch below for a quote tailored to your setup. BigQuery export validation can be added as an optional extra.

#### Best Fit For

- Marketing or analytics teams who suspect their reporting is wrong but can't pin down why.
- Businesses increasing paid media spend who want to trust their conversion data first.
- Mid-size to large e-commerce, retail, or B2C brands with an existing GA4 + GTM setup that nobody has audited since it was first built.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Read/analyst access to GA4.
- Read access to the GTM container (or the tag manager that you are using).
- A contact who can answer questions about business goals and known issues.
