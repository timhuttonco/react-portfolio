### Data Product Manager / Product Owner

**Embedded product ownership for your data platform, not oversight from a distance.**

This is different from the [Fractional Analytics & Data Product Leadership](/services/fractional-analytics-leadership) offer above, which is about owning a whole measurement function or team. This one is hands-on: sitting inside your team as the Product Manager or Product Owner for a specific data product, such as an internal analytics platform, a self-serve reporting tool, a data pipeline, or a measurement roadmap, running discovery, owning the backlog, and working directly with engineers day to day. It's the exact role I held at Kingfisher plc as Analytics & Data Product Manager, where I owned the measurement roadmap, managed a team of Implementation Analysts and Software Engineers, and contributed to a self-serve analytics hub and a unified customer data lake on GCP/BigQuery.

If the data product in question is specifically a CDP roadmap, see [CDP / Tealium AudienceStream Engineering](/services/cdp-tealium-audiencestream-engineering) instead.

**Timeline:** Ongoing, typically 2–5 days/week &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Format:** Interim contract or fractional retainer

#### What's Included

- Embedded product ownership for a defined data product: an internal analytics platform, dashboard suite, data pipeline, or measurement roadmap.
- Discovery with stakeholders and end users to define problems worth solving, not just a backlog of ad hoc feature requests.
- Backlog ownership and prioritisation, written in a way engineers and data teams can actually build against.
- Roadmap definition and communication up to leadership and across functions.
- Day-to-day collaboration with your engineering/data team through planning, standups, and delivery cycles, as much or as little as the engagement calls for.

#### Best Fit For

- Teams building or maintaining an internal data product (an analytics platform, self-serve reporting tool, or data pipeline) without a dedicated PM/PO.
- Businesses covering a hiring gap, parental leave, or an interim need for senior data product ownership.
- Organisations with the engineering capacity but no one translating business needs into a prioritised, sequenced roadmap.

#### What I'll Need From You

- Access to the team and stakeholders the product serves.
- Visibility of any existing roadmap, backlog, or product requirements.
- A defined point of contact for commercial and strategic sign-off.
