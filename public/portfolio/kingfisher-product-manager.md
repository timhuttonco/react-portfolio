### Analytics & Data Product Manager at Kingfisher
#### February 2024 - June 2026

<a href="https://www.kingfisher.com/" target="_blank" title="Kingfisher plc">Kingfisher plc</a> is an international home improvement company with over 1,800 stores across eight countries under well-known brands including <a href="https://www.diy.com/" target="_blank" title="B&Q">B&Q</a>, <a href="https://www.screwfix.com/" target="_blank" title="Screwfix">Screwfix</a>, <a href="https://www.castorama.fr/" target="_blank" title="Castorama">Castorama</a> and <a href="https://www.brico-depot.fr/" target="_blank" title="Brico Dépôt">Brico Dépôt</a>.

As Analytics & Data Product Manager, I owned the Measurement function — accountable for all incoming data across Kingfisher's digital estate, from how it was collected and validated through to how it flowed into downstream platforms and reporting. The role sat at the intersection of product management, data engineering, and commercial strategy, managing a team of Implementation Analysts and Software Engineers.

#### Measurement Strategy & Roadmap

I defined and owned the digital measurement roadmap, aligning data collection frameworks with commercial KPIs across Kingfisher's portfolio of brands. The roadmap operated on a rolling 12-month horizon and shifted the function from reactive delivery to proactive, strategic execution, giving the team and stakeholders clear visibility into priorities, timelines and rationale.

Key milestones included completing the transition to <a href="https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10089681" target="_blank" title="Google Analytics 4">GA4</a> across all banners and directing the migration to <a href="https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/tag-manager/server-side" target="_blank" title="Server-side Google Tag Manager">server-side tagging</a> across the estate, along with migrating from <a href="https://tealium.com/" target="_blank" title="Tealium">Tealium</a> to <a href="https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/tag-manager/" target="_blank" title="Google Tag Manager">Google Tag Manager</a>, materially improving data capture fidelity and ROAS signal quality. This also strengthened privacy compliance as a direct outcome of moving data collection server-side.

#### Data Collection & Quality

Owning the incoming data meant owning its quality. I shipped an automated data quality and consent compliance platform that gave senior stakeholders 24/7 visibility into data integrity and regulatory adherence, ensuring issues were surfaced and resolved before they impacted downstream reporting or decision-making.

I also served as the internal <a href="https://gdpr.eu/" target="_blank" title="GDPR">GDPR</a> compliance SME for data collection, implementing <a href="https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode" target="_blank" title="Advanced Consent Mode V2">Advanced Consent Mode V2</a> across websites and apps, and working closely with legal teams to balance data maximisation with regulatory requirements across multiple European markets.

#### Contribution to Wider Initiatives

Beyond the core measurement remit, I contributed to several broader analytics initiatives within the team. This included supporting the build-out of a Self-Serve Digital Analytics Hub on <a href="https://powerbi.microsoft.com/" target="_blank" title="Power BI">Power BI</a> + <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-gb/microsoft-copilot/microsoft-copilot-for-microsoft-365" target="_blank" title="Microsoft Copilot">Copilot</a>, enabling non-technical stakeholders to query <a href="https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10089681" target="_blank" title="Google Analytics 4">GA4</a> data via natural-language queries and reducing analyst bottlenecks across business units. I was also involved in work towards a unified customer data lake on <a href="https://cloud.google.com/" target="_blank" title="Google Cloud Platform">GCP</a> / <a href="https://cloud.google.com/bigquery" target="_blank" title="BigQuery">BigQuery</a> consolidating Web, App, and CRM data into a single source of truth for Data Science modelling and KPI reporting, as well as integrating data pipelines into <a href="https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai" target="_blank" title="Vertex AI">Vertex AI</a> to support predictive search features on-site and in-app.

#### Team & Stakeholder Management

Led a team of Implementation Analysts and Software Engineers, setting direction, managing priorities, and creating an environment where the team could do their best technical work. Built and maintained strong relationships with stakeholders across Kingfisher's business units — including <a href="https://www.diy.com/" target="_blank" title="B&Q">B&Q</a>, <a href="https://www.castorama.fr/" target="_blank" title="Castorama France">Castorama France</a>, and other Group functions — ensuring measurement work was understood, prioritised, and connected to wider commercial goals.
